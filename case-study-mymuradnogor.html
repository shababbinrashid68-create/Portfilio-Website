MyMuradnogor civic engagement app

User stories and module acceptance criteria for a civic engagement app built with a regional parliamentary team.

Context

MyMuradnogor is a civic engagement app built by Kainotomia Pte Ltd with a regional parliamentary team. The app was planned to connect residents with local civic services and youth programs.

My role

I structured the system requirements as the bridge between the civic stakeholders and the technical team. I wrote the user stories and module acceptance criteria, and contributed input on the youth modules.

Approach

Step 1 Structuring civic requests into user stories and acceptance criteria

I translated high-level feature requests from the regional parliamentary team into structured user stories and module-by-module acceptance criteria that the development team could build and test against.

Decision

Pairing each user story with explicit acceptance criteria gave non-technical civic stakeholders and developers a shared definition of done. [VERIFY]

Step 2 Contributing to the youth modules

The stakeholders wanted youth features, specifically a job portal and sports and esports events. As a member of that age group, I contributed my own input on what those modules should do and wrote their user stories and acceptance criteria.

Decision

Drawing on first-hand perspective from the target age group helped shape practical registration and listing flows for the job portal and sports and esports events. [VERIFY]
