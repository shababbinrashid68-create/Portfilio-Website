About

Business Analyst at Kainotomia Pte Ltd, moving from software services into data-driven product work.

Background

I am a Business Analyst at Kainotomia Pte Ltd, a software services company that builds commercial software and public-sector systems in Bangladesh. My work puts me between non-technical stakeholders (army units, elected representatives, project managers) and the development teams that build for them. I question requirements rather than transcribe them.

I also prepare business proposals—covering technical and financial proposals—and respond to Requests for Proposals (RFPs) and Expressions of Interest (EOIs) for Bangladesh government ICT tenders, including donor-financed projects.

Outside work, I spend time travelling, playing football, jogging and playing video games.

Why a master’s in analytics

I work in software services, where the client defines the product. I want to move into software products, where data-driven decisions determine what gets built. I learned SQL and data visualization through self-directed study and a bootcamp, and I want formal training to take that beyond a foundational level.

I also want to understand AI systems and the statistical concepts behind them.
