About

Business Analyst at Parallaxlogic Infotech, moving from software services into data-driven product work.

Background

I work as a Business Analyst at Parallaxlogic Infotech, a software services company building commercial software and public-sector systems in Bangladesh. Most of my job sits between non-technical stakeholders (army units, elected representatives, project managers) and the developers building for them, which means checking whether a requested rule makes sense in practice before anyone codes it.

I also write technical and financial proposals and respond to Requests for Proposals (RFPs) and Expressions of Interest (EOIs) for Bangladesh government ICT tenders, including donor-financed projects.

Outside work, I spend time travelling, playing football, jogging, and playing video games.

Why a master's in analytics

In software services, clients usually define the scope. I want to move into software products where data guides what gets built.

I learned SQL and data visualization through self-study and a bootcamp. I want formal graduate training to go beyond the basics and build a grounding in the statistical methods behind AI systems.
