Business Analyst at Kainotomia Pte Ltd.

Background

I am a Business Analyst at Kainotomia Pte Ltd, a software services company that builds commercial software and public-sector systems in Bangladesh. My work puts me between non-technical stakeholders, such as army units, elected representatives and project managers, and the development teams that build for them. I test requirements against real cases before they are built, rather than passing them on as written.

I also prepare financial proposals for Bangladesh government ICT tenders, including donor-financed projects.

Why a master’s in analytics

In software services, the client defines the product. I want to work on software products, where data decides what gets built. I learned SQL and data visualization through self-directed study and a bootcamp, and I want formal training to take that beyond a foundational level.

My short-term goal is a Data Analyst or Product Analyst role. My long-term goal is product management.
