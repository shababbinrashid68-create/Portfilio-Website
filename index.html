Questions and answers

Short answers on scope, data governance and technical boundaries across these projects.

Which numbers on this site are scaled or simulated?

In Case Study 1 (WYZR Friends), all user counts are scaled by a confidential multiplier to protect client data, while rates and percentages are unscaled. Because I did not have permission to use live monetization data, all revenue figures and the MRR Scenario parameter are deterministic expected-value simulations assuming a hypothetical $9.99/month tier.

Did you design or train the AI model in Case Study 2?

No. I did not design, train or build the underlying model. My role was evaluating its outputs (threat scores, red flags and summaries), building a Google Sheets findings tracker with Gemini so the engineer could refine the AI engine’s RAG pipeline, testing functional software bugs separately, and contributing input on system functionality.

What is your current SQL and Python proficiency?

My Google BigQuery SQL is at an intermediate level—using CTEs, CASE logic, date functions and GROUP BY / HAVING aggregations—and my Looker Studio work uses parameters for scenario switching. Python is at a beginner level, which is one of the gaps I am applying to graduate analytics programs to close.

Why is the live WYZR Friends dashboard or OSINT platform not linked?

The live WYZR Friends Looker Studio dashboard relies on production BigQuery queries and client data, so it cannot be shared publicly. For the OSINT threat-intelligence platform, my employer granted permission to describe the evaluation methodology only—not to name the platform or display internal records.

What does “questioning requirements rather than transcribing them” look like in practice?

On the Bangladesh Army personnel and equipment management system, an initial requirement fixed the nominated heir share for a soldier’s salary account at 50% for the spouse. Questioning that assumption surfaced cases where a soldier may not be married or may have other dependents, so the rule was agreed and built open and configurable per soldier.
